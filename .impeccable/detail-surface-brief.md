# Detail surface brief

- **Mode and job:** Read mode for the owner or a public visitor opening one indexed item to understand the owner's short judgment and reach its original materials quickly.
- **Content and hierarchy:** The return action participates in the folio's normal flow; the title, one-line deck, and editorial rating mark then share one full-width newspaper-style band. Below it, the short personal evaluation is the primary reading content; one main external link is the primary action; multiple supporting links form the terminal reference index. Metadata includes categories, flat tags, added date, link count, and archive ID. A blank index slip projects from the upper-right edge as physical filing context only.
- **Direction:** An expanded newspaper dossier pulled from the existing Crate Index. Desktop uses a narrow metadata rail beside a broad reading field with a two-column owner note; mobile stacks folio, headline/rating, metadata, judgment, action, and references. The memorable moment is one complete paper record opening inside the graphite tray.
- **Boundaries:** No copied article body, table of contents, recommendations, previous/next navigation, social controls, editing controls, or new visual identity. Search and interface-only language switching remain available.
- **Approved comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/detail-a-approved.png`. The generated stamp and folder geometry guide hierarchy and physicality; all real UI text, links, semantics, and responsive behavior remain authored HTML/CSS.

## Implemented surface contract

- **One-record composition:** The page contains one dossier inside one graphite crate. The existing masthead, search results layer, language switch, footer, palette, textures, and typography continue the homepage world; this surface does not introduce a second identity.
- **Desktop hierarchy (>820px):** The dossier uses a narrow 23.5% metadata rail beside a 76.5% reading field and keeps a 680px minimum height. The folio compresses to 38px with 18px horizontal padding. “返回工具箱” sits in its normal flow at 10px IBM Plex Mono with a 17px short arrow; the remaining archive data uses 9px mono. A full-width headline band then pairs the dominant title/deck with the editorial rating mark; the metadata rail and reading content begin only below that band. Personal judgment, primary source, and reference index follow in that order.
- **Editorial reading rhythm:** The deck uses restrained IBM Plex Sans Condensed beneath a solid top rule. The owner's note uses two balanced serif columns and a fine column rule without a drop cap. The rating character is the only oversized Chinese mark and therefore keeps a higher visual priority than the note. The title remains the dominant full-width event; supporting copy stays compact and must never be added solely to fill the landscape sheet.
- **Approved paper and crate geometry:** The main paper sheet uses `inset: 0`, with one uninterrupted paper texture and four softly rounded corners. The return link remains unboxed IBM Plex Mono text with a short arrow inside the folio's normal flow. The dossier uses `isolation` to enforce three layers: blank right slip at z0, main paper at z1, and all folio/header/body content at z2. On desktop the blank slip is 64px high at `top: 48px` and exposes only 14px beyond the right edge; on mobile it is 50px high at `top: 44px` and exposes only 6px. The slip contains no label or action. The main paper must cover every inward-facing part of it, so it reads as a rear sheet projecting outward—not a notch and never a white overlay patch on the paper face. Preserve the tinted metadata rail, inset crate rim, and paired side grips as a single page-specific composition, not primitives for homepage sleeves.
- **Type roles:** Georgia with Songti-compatible serif fallbacks carries the dossier headline, editorial headings, personal judgment, and supporting explanatory copy. IBM Plex Sans Condensed carries the deck, return/primary actions, and reference titles. IBM Plex Mono carries folio data, archive metadata, indexes, labels, counts, and hosts. `Noto Sans CJK SC Index` is reserved on this surface for the acid rating character only.
- **Editorial rating mark:** Use strong solid rules to contain small edition/label typography and a single acid `夯` grade block. A restrained one-degree print rotation is acceptable. Do not replace it with a circular stamp, pictogram, floating badge, or generic status pill.
- **Single-rule hierarchy:** Detail pages never use `double` borders. Use only solid strokes: 1px hairlines for metadata/reference rows, 2px for secondary section and action boundaries, and 4px for primary editorial divisions such as the headline, judgment, rating, and reference headers. Do not simulate a double rule with adjacent pseudo-elements.
- **Signal hierarchy:** The grade block and primary-source strip are the only acid fields. The primary-source strip is a full-width ruled link within the reading field. Supporting materials end the page as a numbered three-column index, using quiet paper surfaces and hairline dividers.
- **Responsive order (≤820px):** The dossier becomes one column in the order folio → headline/rating → metadata → judgment → primary action → references. The main sheet remains inset 0 above the blank right slip; the slip becomes 50px high at `top: 44px` and exposes only 6px beyond the right edge. The metadata rail becomes a horizontal tinted band, fields use two columns where space permits, the personal note becomes one column, the reference index stacks, and crate side grips disappear. At 520px metadata becomes one column and source-host labels remain hidden.
- **Motion and access:** The dossier opens only on desktop when reduced motion is not requested: 520ms with the established exponential ease-out, a 12px vertical settle, and a left-to-right silhouette reveal. Mobile and reduced-motion modes are static. Return, primary-source, and reference links retain visible hover and keyboard-focus treatment.
- **Terminal boundary:** The reference ledger is the end of the reading flow. Do not append copied article content, a table of contents, recommendations, popularity, previous/next navigation, social controls, editing controls, or authoring affordances.
